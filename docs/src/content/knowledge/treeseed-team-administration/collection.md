The teams collection separates active teams from archived teams and shows the role and operational summaries available to you.

## Active team

The active team scopes Services, Projects, Capacity, Work, and Knowledge. It changes only when you use an explicit **Set active** control or the application selector.

Opening a team overview, settings, membership, or lifecycle page does not silently change this preference.

Archived teams are read-only and cannot remain active.
