Titles should be short and scannable. Slugs are URL-safe identifiers and can usually be generated from the title.

Status describes where a record sits in its workflow. Type or horizon fields describe the kind of record rather than its completion state.

Descriptions and summaries support quick discovery; the Markdown body contains the complete record. Attribution identifies the person or agent responsible for the content.
