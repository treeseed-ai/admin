Use **Triaged** after reviewing scope, **In progress** when work has started, and **Resolved** only when the outcome is known.

Resolution and reopening require a note. Record what changed, why no change was needed, or what new evidence caused the report to reopen. Notes become part of the immutable status timeline.

Status changes do not alter export provenance or erase the original message.
