Knowledge authoring traceability requires every knowledge change to be explainable from its authoring workspace through independent review, exact Git commit, matching TreeDX graph revision, atomic publication manifest, reader output, and derived knowledge packs.

The chain is evidence of one change. Generic read tests or unrelated audit events cannot substitute for correlation to the same content identifiers and source closure.
